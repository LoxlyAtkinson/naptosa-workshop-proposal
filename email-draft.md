**Why the monthly data costs have changed**
- The initial workshop proposal assumed provincial records would share the Western Cape database, with access separated by region. Under that assumption there was no separate storage charge.
- To keep each new entity’s workshop records encrypted, secured and independently separated while still allowing authorised national oversight, the revised option provides nine separately managed data environments: National and the eight new provincial sites. Western Cape keeps its existing environment and is excluded from these new monthly charges.
- Website hosting is R 199 per new site per month (nine sites: R 1,791). Dedicated workshop data is R 499 per new environment per month (nine environments: R 4,491). Together, the dedicated option is R 6,282 per month excluding VAT (R 7,224.30 including VAT). The standard R 699 data rate is reduced to R 499 per environment.
- The original assumption and the two data options are explained in **Workshop data**, PDF page 6. The monthly comparison and service boundaries are in **Monthly costs and service boundaries**, PDF page 9, and in the website chapter **06 | Ongoing services**.
- The Western Cape’s existing site remains in place. The proposed `.org.za` subdomain will route visitors to its current `naptosawc.co.za` website; the Western Cape website and its existing monthly arrangements are outside the new totals. Website maintenance is quoted separately from hosting.
