One plan, built nine times.

The national site and the eight new or rebuilt provincial sites share one plan. Each team then runs its own site.

  • A modern, responsive design in the shared national style, on phone and desktop.
  • Accessible navigation between the national site, the provincial site and the workshop pages.
  • Templates for news, resources, contact details and local pages.
  • Publishing workflows and staff permissions for each provincial team.
  • A way into the workshop module from every site.
  • Clear calls to action on every page.

Search foundation, included

  • Every newly built site gets a technical SEO, AEO and GEO foundation.
  • SEO helps search engines find the site. AEO and GEO help answer engines and AI assistants read it correctly.
  • In practice: a crawlable structure, metadata, structured information and clear organisation signals.
  • We normally charge R 8,000 to R 12,000 per newly built site for this work. Here it carries no extra build charge.
  • We cannot guarantee rankings or placement in search engines or AI answers.

Before

From the first plan to the certificate.

The workshop module already runs in Western Cape. Each province gets it, adapted to its own environment and linked to the national view.

Creators and Finance work with separate permissions.

Create

In use To be built
  • Staff enter the title, date, mode, venue, prices, places, CPTD points and an image.
  • They preview the workshop, publish it and share its booking link or QR code.
  • Creators manage coupon codes, including free places and limited-use discounts.

Book

In use
  • Teachers book without creating an account.
  • Individuals, schools and companies can all book.
  • Staff set member and non-member prices and the number of places.
  • Members give their NAP number or South African ID number.
  • Non-members give a South African ID number, and the form checks that it is a valid 13-digit number.
  • A school can pay for several places, receive a code and register named teachers against them.
  • Staff see how many places are used and how many are still free.

Pay

In use Extending To be built
  • Each EFT booking gets bank instructions and its own payment reference, on screen and by email.
  • Only Finance-authorised staff can confirm a payment.
  • Once Finance records it, the payer receives a combined invoice and receipt PDF.
  • Finance matches references to the bank statement, sees what is paid or outstanding, and can preview or resend receipts.

Attend

In use To be built
  • Each event has a check-in QR code; staff can open check-in early and close it again.
  • Attendees can check themselves in.
  • Staff mark attendance one by one or in a batch.
  • Paid attendees marked present receive numbered certificates.

Follow up

Your data questions

Who can see what?

Provincial staff see only their own province's workshop records. National staff have authorised oversight across provinces.

Creator and Finance permissions are separate, so only Finance can confirm payments.

What happens to the data we already have?

Before anything moves, we inventory the existing content and records and agree a signed mapping: what moves, what is archived and who approves it.

We migrate only approved data.

Who owns the domains and the data?

NAPTOSA does, under either option.

We document administrator access and an exit and export process, and hand both over with the system.

How is the data kept secure?

In the current module, creator and Finance permissions are separate and every money change is logged.

In the network, each province's access is scoped to its own records.

Option B environments are encrypted and backed up. Under Option A, the shared environment is checked against the agreed requirements first.

Before migration we document the platform, hosting region, retention, backup recovery targets, access roles and cross-border processing terms, and validate them with NAPTOSA's information and security owners.

A staged rollout

The provinces light up here in an illustrative order. The real order is agreed after the access review.

  1. Access and inventory

    NAPTOSA names the domain owner and current providers. We send the formal hosting access request and catalogue the existing national, Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal and Western Cape sites, with any records to migrate.

  2. Design and national foundation

    Together we agree the content model, permissions, brand system, national oversight needs and the domain and subdomain map.

  3. Provincial batches

    We build two or three provinces at a time. Between batches we check phone and desktop use, provincial independence, cross-site navigation, security boundaries and national visibility.

  4. Workshop activation

    We set up each agreed workshop environment with the chosen data option, migrate approved data, train staff and test every step from booking to reporting before go-live.

  5. Handover

    You receive administrator access, documentation, the backup and export process, and the monthly service start date.

  • The pace is about one website or province a week, once access, content and design approval are in place. Some take longer, some less.
  • We are working toward the new website network running by early January 2027, and we plan the workshop rollout with you in parallel.
  • This is an estimate, not a promised completion date. A dated sequence follows once we have reviewed access, migration and dependencies.
