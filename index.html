01 | The decision

A single programme, clearly costed

NAPTOSA asked for the full picture: what each province receives, what the workshop module adds, who hosts and maintains the network, how national sees information, and what it costs now and each month.

This network covers a national mother site and nine provincial destinations: Eastern Cape, Free State, Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal, Limpopo, Mpumalanga, North West, Northern Cape and Western Cape. NAPTOSA National accepts and pays for the new programme. Western Cape already has a website, domain, infrastructure and a paid workshop build. Its existing work is retained and carries no new website or workshop build charge in the recommended scope.

Three separate decisions: the once-off website and workshop build, monthly website hosting, and the choice of shared or dedicated workshop-data infrastructure. Training is billed by the number of people trained.

Booking and payment

  • Each published workshop has a shareable booking link and QR code. Teachers can book without creating an account; individuals, schools and companies are supported.
  • Staff set member and non-member prices, capacity and coupon codes, including free places or limited-use discounts.
  • Each EFT booking receives bank instructions and its own payment reference on screen and by email. Finance can match the reference to a bank statement and see paid or outstanding status.
  • A school can pay for multiple places, receive a code and register named teachers against those paid places. Staff can see used versus available places.
  • When Finance records payment, the payer receives a combined invoice and receipt PDF. A paid attendee receives the workshop reminder before the event. Members can find workshop documents in their member portal.

Staff and Finance controls

  • Creators enter title, date, mode, venue, prices, places, CPTD points and an image, then preview and publish. They can share the link/QR and manage coupons.
  • Super admins assign Workshop creator and Finance permissions. Creators can work without seeing money columns; only Finance-authorised staff can confirm EFT payment.
  • Staff export booking, payment, school-place and attendance registers for one or multiple workshops. Finance sees money columns; a creator without Finance permission receives a restricted export.
  • A practice or unpaid workshop can be deleted with a reason. Once anyone has paid, it is closed and the historical records are retained.
  • Finance can inspect payment references and status, preview or resend receipts, and read the money-change audit history.

At the workshop and afterwards

Each event has a check-in QR. Staff can open check-in early, mark attendance individually or in a batch, and issue numbered certificates to paid attendees marked present. Self check-in lets an attendee find and confirm their record. Present attendees receive a thank-you email; absent attendees can receive a missed-you email. Staff can export the attendance register.

Work still being completed in the existing programme: a next-morning staff summary and register reminder; notification when a workshop date, time or venue changes; part-payment balances on reminders; and a school email listing teachers registered through its code. The five added items above keep their stated in-use or to-be-built position. Rollout acceptance will verify each item rather than assume it is already live.

08 | Delivery and terms

A staged transition with visible checkpoints

1 | AccessInventory and approvals
2 | BuildAbout one site per week
3 | VerifyTest roles, data and journeys
4 | HandoverWork toward early January 2027
  1. Access and inventory: NAPTOSA identifies the domain owner and current providers; we issue the formal hosting-access request and catalogue the existing national, Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal and Western Cape sites and any records to migrate.
  2. Design and national foundation: agree the content model, permissions, brand system, national oversight requirements and domain/subdomain map.
  3. Provincial batches: build two or three at a time, check desktop/mobile journeys, provincial autonomy, cross-site navigation, security boundaries and national visibility between batches.
  4. Workshop activation: configure each agreed workshop environment and data option, migrate approved data, train staff and verify booking-to-reporting journeys before go-live.
  5. Handover: provide administrator access, documentation, backup/export process and monthly service start date.

Estimated pace: approximately one website or province per week once access, content and design approval are in place. Some may take longer and others less. We will work toward having the new website network running by early January 2027 and plan the workshop rollout with NAPTOSA in parallel. This is an estimate, not a promised completion date; a dated sequence follows the access, migration and dependency review.
