Created on one form, published with one link

Office staff create a workshop on one form: the title, the dates, in person, online or hybrid, the venue, the member and non-member price, the number of places, the CPTD points and a picture. They preview it, publish it and get a booking link to share.

Pick a province to see what it gets. Every price on this page is per province, paid by NAPTOSA National.

The Western Cape already runs the workshop module, so it is left out of the pricing in this proposal. Pick any other province to see what it gets.

NAPTOSA a province does not run the module yet. If NAPTOSA National includes this province, we set up its workshops, its staff logins and its bank account for EFT payments. The provinces are set up one after another, about 5 working days each, in an order agreed with National.

Bookings and payments, captured.

Teachers, schools and companies book through the link without needing an account. Every booking and every payment is recorded against the person, so the office sees who has paid and who still owes.

Anyone who chooses EFT gets the bank details and their own payment reference on screen and by email. The same reference shows on the staff row, so Finance can match it to the bank statement.

A Finance tick on a staff account lets that person mark an EFT as paid, which emails the invoice and receipt at once. Workshop creators without the tick cannot mark anything paid.

Once NAPTOSA a province is set up, its EFT instructions carry its own bank account.

After the day.

Staff issue a numbered certificate to everyone who paid and was marked present, and it is emailed to them. People marked present get a thank-you email; people marked absent get a "We missed you" email.

Staff download an Excel workbook for one workshop or many: bookings, payments, school places with the teachers who used them, and an attendance register. A creator without the Finance tick gets the same file with the money columns removed.

A summary report emailed to staff the morning after a workshop ends is being built.

The five items we are adding.

At the demo and in our meetings we came across a few things that need a bit more development. So we are adding five items. Two are already in use; three are still to be built. Each added item reaches every province as it is finished.

All five come with the module for NAPTOSA a province.

  • The dedicated auditor portal.To be builtA separate read-only login for auditors. They can view and export the workshop money records, but cannot change anything.
  • The built-in approval methods for publishing a workshop and changing any details, including banking details.To be builtA named approver approves a workshop before it goes public. New bank details take effect only after a second person approves them. Approval for other detail changes, such as a date, venue or price, is new work within this item.
  • The automated audit trail.In use for money changes, being extendedEvery money change is logged with who made it and when. It is being extended to more of the system.
  • The self-check-in feature that we created extra.In useIn the Western Cape today. You saw it on the day itself.
  • The on-site or on-premises invoicing system for walk-ins who need to be registered and paid.To be builtRegister the person, record the payment, mark them present and email the receipt, in one step at the door.
